PACRA Limited Company Registration

Fee: K3,500

Requirements

1. Name Clearance

You suggest three names, and one will be approved.

2. Directors Details

Minimum of two directors with the following info:

  • Full Names of Director
  • Email addresses & Contact Numbers
  • NRC Numbers & Date of Birth
  • Residential address & nearby Road name
  • Expected business start date & Financial year end
  • Front and Back Copies of NRC

Upon Completion

The PACRA Certificate is issued immediately upon completion.

PACRA Business Registration

Fee: K1,200

Requirements

1. Name Clearance

Suggest three names to be approved by PACRA.

2. Owner / Director Details

Provide the following info:

  • Full Names, Email, and Phone Contact
  • NRC Number & Date of Birth
  • Residential Address & Nearby Road Name
  • Business Start Date & Financial Year End
  • Front and Back Copies of NRC

Upon Completion

The PACRA Certificate and ZRA T-PIN Number Certificate are issued immediately upon completion.
